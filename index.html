Hello There!

I'm a full-stack developer at Microsoft in Vancouver, working across PowerPoint Live, Teams, and Windows Photos. Most of what I do sits where front-end performance meets real-time media — making large web apps load faster, stream more reliably, and fail more gracefully when the network doesn't cooperate.

I started out at AltumView building IoT and computer-vision systems, where I'm a named inventor on a patent for privacy-preserving fall detection. Since joining Microsoft in 2020 I've taken a hackathon prototype to 3.7 million monthly active users, led a cross-org migration of PowerPoint Live's sync pipeline, and built a lot of the inner-loop tooling my team uses day to day. I'm currently working through Stanford's XCS221 on artificial intelligence.
